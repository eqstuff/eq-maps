Notes

Directions. N, E, S, W follow the in-game map, with north at the top of the map window. Numbers are approximate distances in map units, and each leg is simplified from the walkable corridors, so follow the corridor rather than a rigid straight line. Locations are /way Y, X, Z commands: click one to copy it, paste it into the chat window and EverQuest sets a waypoint. The conversion from the map file follows the community-documented format (map x = −second /loc number, map y = −first); it hasn’t been verified in the client, so stand on one stair and type /loc to check before trusting the compass.

Limits. Routes are the shortest walk computed from the wall outlines, assuming every gap in a wall is a doorway. The map has no doors, so a wall gap that’s really a barrier would break a route. The Teritarx Preservation Chamber is a closed outline on the map, so its route ends at its walls. The Magicae Sanctuary cave has no closed outline, so its directions are rougher. The Teleport and the pit connect to the tower only through the teleport link T1 (central hub), which comes from in-game experience, not from the map, and is assumed to work both ways. The +468 sky platform isn’t included. “Crasis Whesar” and “Keeper of the Matrix” may be NPC labels rather than rooms.
