Arx Mentis: floors and connections

Decoded from Good’s map files (arxmentis.txt and its _1, _2, _3 label layers). The zone has 13 floor heights (three are small landings or annexes) plus a sky platform nothing connects to, joined by 20 ramps and stairs. The diagram shows what links to what; the cards below show each floor’s layout, where each link starts and ends, and what’s on it.

What connects to what

Click a floor box (or a room inside it) to set the start, then click another for the destination. The links you need light up, everything else fades, and the steps appear alongside. A third click starts over.

Notes on reading this

Coordinates. Plans are drawn in raw map-file orientation. Locations are given as /way Y, X, Z commands: click one to copy it, paste it into the chat window and EverQuest sets a waypoint you can follow. A map point (x, y) is converted to the in-game Y, X order as (−y, −x), the same order /loc prints. This hasn’t been verified in the client, so spot-check one stair by standing on it and typing /loc before trusting the orientation.

How I decoded it. Each L line is a wall segment x1,y1,z1,x2,y2,z2,r,g,b; each P line is a label x,y,z,r,g,b,size,text. Floors are groups of flat segments at the same Z. A stair is a bundle of sloped segments joining two floors. _1 holds the in-zone labels. _2 holds the key (ground-spawn and Hunter placeholder legend), drawn far outside the zone at x up to 2030, so I excluded it from the plans.

What the file can’t tell you. It has no doors, zone lines, teleport destinations or elevator logic. The way into the sky platform is unknown, and the teleport link (T1) is added from in-game experience rather than read from the map. Floor names come from Good’s room labels (Teritarx / Protarx / Anetarx / Penutarx look like four tiers, low to high); where a floor had no label it is described by what it connects. Directions are straight lines between points: Good’s outlines have gaps, so the walls aren’t used to find a path, except on the two floors with several rooms (+80, +172), which I divided into rooms and corridors by hand.
